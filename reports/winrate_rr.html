Win rate vs reward: where the bot sits

Every point on the map is a possible strategy: how often it wins (left to right) and how big the average win is compared with the average loss (bottom to top). The colour is what it earns per trade, in multiples of the risk (R): blue makes money, red loses, grey is breakeven. The dots are the bot's real trades (TBO v2.8, after fees and funding).

The numbers behind the dots

Win = a trade closed above 0R. Average loss includes the many breakeven exits (stop moved to entry at +1.5R, then hit: about −0.05R after fees), which is why it is below 1R. Win rate × average win − loss rate × average loss = the real average per trade.

How to read it

  • The solid line is breakeven: to make money at a win rate p you need a reward-to-risk above (1 − p) ÷ p. At 50% that is 1, at 30% it is 2.3, at 17% (this bot) it is 4.9. The bot's is about 9, so it sits far above the line.
  • Dashed lines mark +0.5R and +1R per trade. The bot earns about +0.5–0.6R per trade on average: every trade is, on average, worth half of what it risks.
  • Strategies high on the right (win often and win big) barely exist; markets do not offer them for long. Most real strategies live near the line: either many small wins (right, low) or few big wins (left, high). This bot is a "few big wins" strategy, which is why it has long losing runs.
  • The vertical axis is logarithmic so both small and large ratios fit.
