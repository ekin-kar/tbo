TBO crypto bot statistics

Every trade of TBO v2.6 (4-hour trend breakout, long and short) on BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL and ADA on Binance USD-M futures, February 2020 (first trade) to September 2026, after fees, slippage and funding. Percent and dollar figures run one account trading the selected coins together, sized the way the bot sizes: the risk ladder (BTC 6% / ETH 4% of the balance under $1,000, 5% / 3.3% up to $10,000, 2.5% / 1.7% above; alts 0.83%, from $10,000 0.42%) with the open-risk cap (BTC never shrunk), shorts at half size. How these numbers were made

  • Trades come from a 1-minute backtest engine on Binance futures prices (last price, 1-minute candles). The bot's own code was run against a simulated exchange on the same history: on BTC 327 of 328 trades matched to the minute, on ETH 316 of 320 (the rest are minutes where the backtest assumes the stop is hit inside the fill minute, which is pessimistic).
  • Costs: taker fee 0.05% per side, plus slippage 0.01% (BTC, ETH) or 0.03% (BNB, SOL, ADA) on every stop or market fill; funding 0.01% per 8 hours charged on every open position (long or short), which is pessimistic.
  • R figures (win rate, profit factor, net R, drawdown in R) are pure trade statistics: they do not depend on risk, coin weights or the short-size factor. The combined R columns add all selected coins' trades at 1R each.
  • Dollar and percent figures run one account through time, sized like the bot (v2.5): each trade risks its coin's % from the ladder row for the balance at entry (× the short-size factor for shorts); an ETH / BNB / SOL / ADA trade is shrunk so the risk of all open trades not yet at breakeven stays within the cap (1.1× that row's BTC risk), or skipped if less than a quarter fits; BTC is never shrunk but its risk counts. A trade's result is size × R at its exit. The worst-case drawdown counts every open trade not yet at breakeven as lost at its stop. The bot's five bots were run together on a simulated account and matched this rule on every trade.
  • A win is a trade closed above +0.2R, a loss below −0.2R, the rest are breakeven exits (the stop was moved to the entry at +1.5R).
  • The open-interest filter (v2.4) only acts from 2022 (Binance's open-interest history starts in Dec 2021) and was chosen after seeing 2022–26: 24 of 32 nearby settings also beat the bot without it in both 2022–23 and 2024–26, and random skipping of the same number of orders never matched it, but expect live gains to be smaller.
  • Honest caveats: the rules were tuned on BTC 2020–26, ADA was picked after seeing 2020–23 and BNB + SOL replaced DOGE in Oct 2026 after seeing their stronger 2024–26 (recent strength helps only a little when choosing coins), and the coin weights and short size were chosen after seeing all years, so the full-history figures are optimistic. The 2024–26 period is the best guide; expect live results somewhat below it. In a test with 200 random-entry versions of the same rules, the real entries beat all 200 in both 2020–23 and 2024–26.
