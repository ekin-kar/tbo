Can open interest spot false breakouts?

A search for anything in Binance's positioning data (open interest, long/short ratios, liquidation flushes) that tells the bot's real 4h breakouts from false ones, tested properly: , every idea as a grid of settings, judged by six gates fixed before any result was seen. The base is today's bot (v2.6 rules with the open-interest filter it already has), so anything that passes adds to what is live now.

How the ideas were judged

Coins in three groups. Dev (where ideas were picked): our 5 + XRP, DOGE, AVAX, LINK, DOT, LTC, TRX. Untouched big coins (14: BCH, XLM, SUI, HBAR, SHIB, PEPE, NEAR, UNI, ETC, APT, AAVE, ATOM, FIL, ICP) and untouched small coins (25), looked at only at the end. Periods: 2022–23 and 2024–26 (open-interest history starts Dec 2021). "Ratio" = profit ÷ worst drop, in R (shorts count half, as in the bot).

How many settings survived each gate

The one that passed: no order when open interest is near its 90-day low

At each 4h close the bot would rank the coin's open interest (USD value, hourly) against the last 90 days. If it is in the lowest 20%, no breakout order is placed for that candle. Low open interest = few positions open, little leverage, little interest in the coin: a breakout then has no fuel behind it. The current OI filter (OI down ≥3% in 72 h) is about a fall; this one is about the level, so they catch different candles.

It is really about shorts

Open interest sits near its 90-day low mostly after a fall, when everyone has already closed out. That is when the bot's short breakouts happen, and those are the ones it removes: a breakdown with nobody left to be forced out tends to fizzle.
