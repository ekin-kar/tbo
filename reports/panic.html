The panic sleeve

A second strategy next to the trend bot: it buys forced-liquidation crashes and sells the rebound. When a coin falls hard while open interest collapses, leveraged longs are being liquidated: Binance sells their positions at any price, the price overshoots, and it tends to snap back once the forced selling is over. (When open interest rises during a drop, new shorts are opening on purpose: real selling, no rebound.) It runs on a Binance sub-account so its positions never touch the bot's. The numbers below use the bot's real sizing (risk ladder, open-risk cap, compounding) on one balance from $300, with fees, slippage and funding.

How robust is it?

  • Open interest is the signal. On 41 other coins (never used to find the idea), the bigger the open-interest fall, the better the rebound: average per trade +0.2% at −6%, +0.9% at −10%, +1.6% at −12%, +2.7% at −15%, +4.5% at −20%, win rate 51% → 71%. Price falls with rising open interest (new positions, not liquidations) keep falling.
  • Neighbouring settings: of 81 combinations around the chosen one (window 3-6 h, open interest −10/−12/−15%, premium −0.5/−1/−1.5, 2-4 units), 70% give more money and a smaller worst drop than the bot alone in both runs; with 3 units the typical neighbour adds +17% (from 2022) / +6% (from 2024) and 2-3 points less drop. The exact chosen point is one of the better ones: expect the typical neighbour, not the best.
  • Coins it never saw: with the settings frozen first, the 41 other coins (sized per event) improved the bot's return ÷ drop by ×1.03-1.13 (2022-23) and ×1.17-1.21 (2024-26). Settings tuned on the 5 coins alone did not carry over; settings found on the 41 coins did: the plateau, not the 5-coin optimum, is what can be trusted.
  • Random buying does not do this: the same number of long trades at random times (or within a week of the real signals) made the bot worse in 100% of 200 tries; the timing of the forced selling is what matters.
  • Short squeezes (the mirror): rare on these coins (5 in 4¾ years) and losing: long only. Waiting for the bounce to start before buying: worse (the rebound starts at once).
  • Weak spot: long bear-market cascades (2022: LUNA, FTX), where liquidations keep coming for days. Open-interest data starts in Dec 2021, so 2022 is the only bear market in the test. The bot and the sleeve were tested on one balance; the sub-account split is handled by sizing both on main + sub.
