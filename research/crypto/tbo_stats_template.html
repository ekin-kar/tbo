How these numbers were made

  • Trades come from a 1-minute backtest engine on Binance futures prices (last price, 1-minute candles). The bot's own code was run against a simulated exchange on the same history: on BTC 327 of 328 trades matched to the minute, on ETH 316 of 320 (the rest are minutes where the backtest assumes the stop is hit inside the fill minute, which is pessimistic).
  • Costs: taker fee 0.05% per side, plus slippage 0.01% (BTC, ETH) or 0.03% (DOGE, ADA) on every stop or market fill; funding 0.01% per 8 hours charged on every open position (long or short), which is pessimistic.
  • R figures (win rate, profit factor, net R, drawdown in R) are pure trade statistics: they do not depend on risk, coin weights or the short-size factor. The combined R columns add all selected coins' trades at 1R each.
  • Dollar and percent figures run one account through time: each trade's size is its coin's risk % of the balance at entry (× the short-size factor for shorts); a trade's result is size × R at its exit. The worst-case drawdown counts every open trade not yet at breakeven as lost at its stop. No cap on total open risk (the bot has none; at the default settings all four open at once risk about 5%).
  • A win is a trade closed above +0.2R, a loss below −0.2R, the rest are breakeven exits (the stop was moved to the entry at +1.5R).
  • Honest caveats: the rules were tuned on BTC 2020–26, DOGE and ADA were picked after seeing 2020–23, and the coin weights and short size were chosen after seeing all years, so the full-history figures are optimistic. The 2024–26 period is the best guide; expect live results somewhat below it. In a test with 200 random-entry versions of the same rules, the real entries beat all 200 in both 2020–23 and 2024–26.
