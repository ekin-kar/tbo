How to read this

  • Trades come from the 1-minute backtest of the bot's rules (the bot's own code matched it trade for trade on a simulated exchange). Times are UTC.
  • Risk is what the trade would lose at its stop: the ladder's % of the balance at entry (shorts half), possibly cut by the open-risk cap. R is the result in units of that risk (−1R = full stop, +5R = five times the risk), after fees and funding; P&L = risk × R.
  • How it ended: stop-loss (−1R or a bit worse), breakeven stop (the stop had moved to the entry at +1.5R, so about 0R minus costs), profit-lock stop (the trade had reached +5R, so the stop had moved to +1.5R), or the 5-day time exit.
  • Cap: "cut to x%" means the open-risk cap made the trade smaller because other coins (usually BTC) were already in fresh trades; "skipped" means less than a quarter fitted, so the bot took no trade.
  • Price chart: 4-hour candles. Dashed line = entry price; red dashed = the stop until breakeven, then the stop at the entry; the shaded area is the time the trade was open.
  • A backtest of past markets. Real fills will be slightly worse; expect live results somewhat below these.
