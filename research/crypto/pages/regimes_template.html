Crypto market regimes

Every day of BTC and ETH since 2020, sorted into regimes by three methods built on 7 daily features (20-day move in ATR, efficiency, signed 60-day R², ATR14/ATR100, bar overlap, up / down follow-through). RB = readable rules, TS = two speeds (20-day trend × 5-day swing × volatility), HM = 6 clusters of the 7 features. Every threshold and cluster centre was fixed on 2020–23 only; a day's state uses that day's close, so a trade only ever sees the state of the day before it. Then the question: does TBO v3.1 do better or worse in some regimes, and does any regime rule beat v3.1 out of sample?
