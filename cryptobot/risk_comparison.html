Risk comparison: $200 for 2 years

What the bot (TBO v2.5: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, ADA, open-interest filter, shorts at half size, open-risk cap with BTC exempt) could do with $200 over 24 months with its risk ladder (7.5% BTC risk under $1,000, 5% up to $10,000, 2.5% above) and with flat risk levels. Each level was run on the same simulated 2-year market paths, built from random 4-week blocks of the bot's real trades, so the levels are compared fairly. Fees, slippage and funding included.

How to read this

  • Each path glues together random 4-week blocks of the bot's real backtest trades (all 5 coins together, as the bot trades them). The same paths are used for every risk level and deposit, so differences come from the settings, not from luck.
  • "Risk" is the share of the balance lost if a trade's stop is hit: BTC / ETH / each of BNB, SOL, ADA. The ladder (the bot's portfolio.json) picks the row for the current balance at every trade, and goes back up if the balance falls below a step. Every profile uses the open-risk cap (1.1× the BTC risk; BTC never shrunk but counted). Higher BTC risk needs a higher position-size cap (max_leverage) so tight-stop trades are not cut: the bot has 12× for BTC.
  • "Worst drop" counts open trades as if they hit their stop (worst case), from the highest balance reached so far.
  • This is a backtest of past markets, with the open-interest filter and BNB/SOL chosen partly after seeing 2022-26. Real results are usually worse than a backtest: read the chances as optimistic. A market unlike 2022-26 can do worse than every path here.
  • Below about $100 some alt trades become smaller than Binance's minimum order and are skipped, so very small balances recover more slowly than shown.
